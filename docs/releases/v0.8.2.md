# Kimi-GUI 0.8.2

Version 0.8.2 is a polish pass: the surfaces that used to appear out of
nowhere now animate, and the transcript rhythm is tightened around long
thinking blocks.

## Motion where things used to teleport

Guided by Emil Kowalski's animation skills
([emilkowalski/skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills)), every
surface was swept with a four-question gate (frequency, purpose, speed,
function). Five spots earned animation, all on the existing easing
token, transform and opacity only:

- **Settings and Skills modals** pop in (180ms) instead of only fading
  the backdrop.
- **Composer dropdowns** (model, thinking, permission) scale in from
  their trigger — and from the correct corner when they open upward.
- **The group-unassign drop zone** rises from the bottom edge while
  dragging, with a faster exit than entrance.
- **Composer option pills and the change-status strip** fade in quietly
  instead of snapping into the row.

Just as many candidates were deliberately rejected: chat messages,
session list rows, and high-frequency pills stay still — things you see
a hundred times a day should not move. Reduced motion keeps the fades
and drops the movement.

![Settings modal captured mid pop-in animation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kaminion/Kimi-GUI/v0.8.2/docs/media/release-0.8.2-motion-modal.png)

## Tighter transcript rhythm

Long thinking blocks used to inflate the space between conversations.
The message gap is now 16px between rows (was 20px) and 24px before a
new turn (was 32px), and thinking paragraphs pack at 8px instead of
12px — secondary voice stays denser than the primary answer.

![Transcript with tightened spacing around thinking blocks](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kaminion/Kimi-GUI/v0.8.2/docs/media/release-0.8.2-transcript-rhythm.png)
