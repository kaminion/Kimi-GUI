# Kimi-GUI 0.8.8

Version 0.8.8 fixes disappearing conversation history with proper paged
loading, and covers the remaining loading gaps with skeleton UI.

## Older messages scroll in infinitely

A session used to load a single page of messages, so long conversations
silently dropped everything older — the daemon paginates with a `before_id`
cursor, and Kimi-GUI never asked for page two.

History now opens at the newest page and pages backwards as you scroll to the
top, on both engines (the local stores page identically to the daemon). A
compact skeleton marks the fetch at the top of the transcript, and the
viewport stays anchored to the message you were reading — no jump when the
older page lands.

![Older-history skeleton at the top of the transcript while paging back](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kaminion/Kimi-GUI/v0.8.8/docs/media/release-0.8.8-history-older-skeleton.png)

## Loading gaps get skeletons

Opening the side panel mid-run hid the **작업** (Tasks) section until the
first task-list fetch landed. It now keeps its header and shows three pulsing
skeleton rows in the same visual language as the sidebar and transcript
skeletons, so the section never pops in late.

![Task-list skeleton rows under the running status strip](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kaminion/Kimi-GUI/v0.8.8/docs/media/release-0.8.8-panel-tasks-skeleton.png)

## Verification
